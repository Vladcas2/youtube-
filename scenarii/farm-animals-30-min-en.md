## 2. Segment template

Each animal runs **~45–55 seconds** and follows the same recipe, so it feels predictable to kids:

| Step | Duration | On screen | Audio |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 5 s | **Cartoon/animated** animal enters the frame | Soft music |
| 2 | 5 s | Same animal, animated | Narrator: "Look! Who is this?" |
| 3 | 10 s | **Real footage** of the animal | Name spoken clearly and slowly: "Cow." |
| 4 | 10 s | Real footage + **name written** at the bottom in big letters (CAPITALS, rounded font) | The animal's real sound |
| 5 | 10 s | Real footage, different angle | One simple sentence about the animal |
| 6 | 5–10 s | Name in the center on a white background | Narrator repeats the name + pause so the child can repeat |

Style rules:
- Warm, slow voice with 2–3 second pauses after each name.
- At most one short sentence per animal (5–8 words).
- No fast cuts – at least 4–5 seconds per shot.
- Very quiet background music, no lyrics.

## 4. Production notes

**What you need:**
- **Real animal footage:** film it yourself at a farm or village (best – 100% original content)
  or use free stock with a commercial license (Pexels, Pixabay, Videvo).
- **Animal sounds:** Freesound.org (check for CC0 license), Pixabay Sounds, or record them yourself.
- **Animations:** simple 2D illustrations (Canva, Vyond, Animaker) with gentle motion
  (enter/exit, blinking, swaying).
- **Background music:** YouTube Audio Library (free, no Content ID claims).
- **Voice:** a native or near-native English speaker, or a high-quality AI voice
  (use a warm, slow, friendly style).
- **Font:** rounded and bold (e.g. Baloo 2, Fredoka), big letters, high contrast.

**Avoid:**
- Copying clips, animations or voice from the original video → risk of a copyright strike.
- "Reused content" (just stock clips stitched together without your own voice/animation) → risk
  of being rejected from the YouTube Partner Program. Your own narration and animation protect you.
- Fast transitions, harsh sounds, flashing colors.

**Competition note:** the English "farm animals for kids" niche is crowded. The "Mommies and babies"
and "Guess the shadow" chapters are your main differentiators – consider putting them in the
title or thumbnail (e.g. "Farm Animals and Their Babies").

**Ideas for the series (next videos, same format):**
- Forest animals
- Jungle / zoo animals
- Farm animals part 2 (turkey, buffalo, llama, pigeon)
- Vehicles and their sounds
- Learn colors with animals
